# J.A.R.V.I.S. Disclaimers

**Version:** v1.0  
**Effective date:** 30 August 2026

## AI Disclaimer

J.A.R.V.I.S. responses may be inaccurate, incomplete, out of date, or unsuitable for a particular purpose. AI systems can hallucinate, misunderstand commands, produce unsafe suggestions, or behave differently across cloud providers, local phone models, desktop llama.cpp models, GGUF quantizations, and Offline Core rules. Cloud providers may process requests under their own terms. Local processing can reduce transmission to cloud services but does not guarantee correctness or privacy in every configuration.

Do not automatically treat J.A.R.V.I.S. output as fact. For important medical, legal, financial, safety, identity, security, or other consequential decisions, verify information with appropriate authoritative sources and qualified professionals.

## Device Control Disclaimer

J.A.R.V.I.S. can control a device only where the device is supported, configured, authorized, properly paired, enabled, and reachable through an available transport. Same-network visibility, Bluetooth visibility, or discovery does not grant trust. Operating-system permissions, device state, third-party APIs, network failures, model mistakes, and configuration errors can prevent a command from working or can produce an unexpected result.

Potentially destructive operations must require an explicit confirmation step. Natural-language ambiguity must never directly execute a dangerous action.

```text
Shutdown PC?
[ CANCEL ] [ CONFIRM ]

Delete this file?
[ CANCEL ] [ DELETE ]
```

Users must review the target device, action, and consequence before confirming. Unsupported or unauthorized actions must be denied rather than silently approximated.
